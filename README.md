# ⚡ DevMind Focus: Complete User Guide

Welcome to **DevMind Focus**—an ultra-premium, gamified developer tool designed to eliminate distractions and build deep work habits through hardware-bound licensing, silent background enforcement, and interactive focus widgets.

---

## 🚀 Quickstart Guide

Getting started with DevMind Focus is incredibly simple and takes less than 30 seconds:

1. **Launch the Dashboard**: Double-click [**`release/devmind.exe`**](file:///c:/Users/aarav.SOMEGUYWITHPRET/FocusFlow/release/devmind.exe). A beautiful, high-contrast matte-black desktop control dashboard will launch immediately.
2. **Start the Watcher Daemon**: Click **`Start Daemon`** in the dashboard control panel. The background scanning loop is now active, running with **0% measurable CPU**!
3. **Trigger your First Interception**: Open your browser and navigate to a distracting site like `youtube.com` or `reddit.com`. The screen will immediately lock into a full-screen taking-over experience!
4. **Complete a Breathing Cycle**: Follow the custom-fading ocean wave sound and breathing indicator for 10 seconds. Click **`Back to Focus`**—the distracting tab will close instantly, and you will be awarded **+15 XP**!

## 🖥️ Core Dashboard Features

### 1. The Real-Time Stats Overview
At the top of the dashboard, you will find your gamified stats:
* **Operator Level**: Displays your current focus level. 
* **Focus Experience (XP)**: Shows your exact XP and how much is required to progress to your next rank.
* **Neutralized Blocks**: Counters how many distractions you successfully averted today in real-time.

### 2. Sleek Corner Focus Sessions (Floating Widget)
When you are ready to begin a dedicated coding session:
* Type a **Mission Objective** (e.g. `Refactoring Auth Pipeline`) into the Focus Session card.
* Click **`Launch Focus Mode`**.
* The main dashboard window will instantly shrink and lock to the bottom-right corner of your screen as a beautiful **floating topmost micro-widget** (`280x220` pixels).
* The widget displays a live elapsed timer, your mission objective, and a counter of how many distractions you resisted during this specific session.
* Click **`Expand`** to bring the full dashboard back, or **`End Session`** to finish. Ending your session calculates your focus time and awards you **`+10 XP` per minute** focused!

### 3. Managing your Blocklist
Under the **Blocklist** tab, you can customize what DevMind blocks:
* **Distracting Websites**: Type any domain keyword (e.g., `reddit`, `facebook`, `instagram`) and click **`Add Item`**. If any foreground tab or window contains this keyword in its title, DevMind will intercept it immediately.
* **Distracting Desktop Apps**: Type the exact process file name (e.g., `Spotify.exe`, `Discord.exe`) and click **`Add Item`**. DevMind will instantly terminate the app if it is active while you are working!

### 4. Personal Settings & Pro Upgrades
Under the **Settings & Pro** tab:
* **Sound Toggle**: Click to mute or unmute the soothing, low-volume procedural wave audio during taking over.
* **Cryptographic Pro Activation**: Locate your secure, hardware-bound **Machine ID**. Enter your signed Pro license key and click **`Activate Pro`** to unlock unlimited daily interceptions! (Free tier is limited to 100).

## 💻 Headless CLI for Power Users

If you prefer to operate entirely from your command line terminal, DevMind Focus supports high-performance commands:

* **Start Background Watcher**:
  ```powershell
  .\release\devmind.exe start
  ```
  *(Launches the watcher daemon silently. No console pop-up will remain on your screen.)*

* **Check Telemetry Status**:
  ```powershell
  .\release\devmind.exe status
  ```
  *Output:*
  ```text
  daemon          : running    (pid 26404)
  interceptions   : 1 / unlimited (today | pro)
  license         : pro
  ```

* **Stop Background Watcher**:
  ```powershell
  .\release\devmind.exe stop
  ```
